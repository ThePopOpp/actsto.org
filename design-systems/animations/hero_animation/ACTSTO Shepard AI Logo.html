ACTSTO · Sub-brand identity

Shepard AI

An icon & logo for ACTSTO’s new AI agent. Built on the biblical shepherd’s crook — the staff that leads, gathers, and keeps the flock — and a woolly sheep drawn from a sideways “S” — each set inside the ACTSTO disc so it reads as family. Five directions below, in light, dark, and single-colour.

Left: the pure crook — timeless, reduces to a favicon. Right: the crook cradling a guiding light in its curl — the shepherd’s lamp doubling as the spark of an intelligent agent. Gold marks the light throughout.

The full set

Four ways to tell the same story.

Each keeps the ACTSTO disc and the shepherd’s crook. What changes is what the staff is doing — leading, lighting, gathering, or keeping.

§ 07

New direction · E

A sheep, built from a sideways S.

Turn the “S” of Shepard on its side and the two bowls become a woolly back. Add a head, an ear, and little legs — and the letter is a sheep. The wordmark’s own initial becomes the flock it tends.
