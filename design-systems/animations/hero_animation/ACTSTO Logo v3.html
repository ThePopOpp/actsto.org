§ 01

The Refined Badge

Same composition, more dimension.

A radial gradient gives the disc volume. The cross becomes a beveled object with a soft drop-shadow into the book. The book gains a volumetric base, faint page lines, and a center fold the cross descends into.

§ 02

Isometric Direction

The disc tilts. The cross rises. The book rests on top.

The whole composition lifts into an isometric view — the disc becomes a shallow cylinder, the cross extrudes as a 3D block, and the book sits on the disc's top surface. A literal “raised emblem.”

§ 03

Negative Space Direction

The cross and book are not drawn — the disc opens for them.

A solid red disc with the cross and open book cut out as one continuous negative space. Faith and education are what the badge makes room for. Holds shape at any size and reproduces cleanly on any background.
