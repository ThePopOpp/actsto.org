Identity v2 · Book + Cross Direction

ACTSTO

Two design directions for the ACTSTO mark — each rooted in a closed book and a Christian cross. Isometric, where the book sits in dimensional space and the cross is on the cover. Negative space, where the cross is cut out of the book itself. Each direction in flat and 3D, on light and dark.

§ 01

The Two Directions

A book and a cross — two ways to render the same idea.

A closed Christian book carries education, scripture, and stewardship in one mark. Whether to put the cross on the book (isometric) or through it (negative space) is the brand decision.

Direction A · Isometric · Flat

The book sits in space. The cross sits on its cover.

Two-color isometric rendering. Three faces visible: cover (navy), top of pages, and fore-edge. The cross is on the cover — long vertical, short horizontal — aligned with the book's local axes.

Direction A · Isometric · 3D

Same book — with light, page edges, a shadow.

3D rendering adds linear gradients (lit from upper-right), page-edge striations on the fore-edge, a beveled highlight on the cross, and a soft ground shadow. Use when the brand wants depth — campaign hero art, app marquees, gala invites.

§ 04

Direction B · Negative Space · Flat

The cross is not drawn — the book opens around it.

A front-view book silhouette with a Christian cross cut as a hole. The negative space carries the meaning: the cross is what the book makes room for. Highly reducible — works at favicon size with no detail loss.

§ 05

Direction B · Negative Space · 3D

The book has depth — the cross reveals a red interior.

Extruded version. The cover sits in front, the top and fore-edges of pages show above and to the right, and the cross cut-out reveals a Liberty Red interior — as if the book itself is full of the gift inside. Use for large-format and dimensional applications.

Wordmark

ACTSTO — one word, combined letterforms.
