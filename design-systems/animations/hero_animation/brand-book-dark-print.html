§ 01

Foundation

The brand begins with a civic act — not a slogan.

Mission

We connect Arizona taxpayers with Christian families so a state tax obligation becomes a child's tuition — at no extra cost to the donor.

  • Steward
  • Connect
  • Disclose
  • Repeat

Brand Pillars

i.

Trust by structure

Every dollar is traced. Statute, school, student, receipt. The visual system mirrors the audit trail — clean rules, clear hierarchy.

ii.

Faith without volume

Christian conviction shows in language and care, not crosses and flags. Restraint is reverence.

iii.

Civic, not partisan

Patriotic palette, editorial voice. We are Arizonans funding Arizonans through Arizona law.

iv.

Family at the center

Numbers serve a name. Every campaign has a child, a parent, a school — never a statistic alone.

Voice

We sound like a trusted neighbor who happens to know the statute.

We are

Plainspoken. Specific with numbers. Warm about families. Quietly confident about the law.

We are not

Salesy. Politicized. Sentimental. Vague about money. Loud about faith.

Sample line

“Your April 15 obligation can pay Maya's September tuition. Same dollar. Different destination.”

The italic emphasis rule

One word, sometimes two. Set in Fraunces italic, colored Liberty Red, never bolder than the surrounding weight. The eye should pause, not stop.

Redirect your tax.

A child's future, on file.

Stewardship, made simple.

Redirect your tax and a child's future on file.

Three emphases in one line reads as marketing. Pick one.

A.R.S. §43-1089 · The mechanism

Same dollar.
Different destination.

Arizona's individual tax credit is the most efficient act of educational generosity in the state. You redirect what you already owe — the state credits you back, dollar for dollar, when you file.

  1. 01

    Choose a campaign

    Browse families currently fundraising. Filter by school, grade, or county. Read the story. Verify the school.

  2. 02

    Redirect your tax

    Donate up to your cap. The funds go to the certified School Tuition Organization (STO) that supports the campaign.

  3. 03

    Receive your receipt

    Within 24 hours, an STO receipt arrives by email. Suitable for filing with your Arizona return.

  4. 04

    Claim the credit

    File the receipt. The state credits you dollar‑for‑dollar against your Arizona tax liability.

  5. 05

    Funds disburse

    The STO sends the funds to the partner school after enrollment verification. The family is notified. You are notified.

A note to whoever holds this next

Restraint is the brand.

If a screen feels loud, remove a color. If a sentence feels sold, remove an adjective. If an italic word feels decorative, remove it. The platform is asking people to file paperwork on behalf of children. The aesthetic should match the act.
